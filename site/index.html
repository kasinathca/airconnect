04 / SOURCES & REPRODUCIBILITY

Research methodology

Data provenance, processing decisions, validation strategy, limitations and sources.

1. Data and provenance

The project pins three India Aviation Stats tables derived from DGCA workbooks: 13,599 domestic airport-month rows, annual domestic/international traffic, and quarterly international traffic. The source commit and SHA-256 hashes are stored in the generated analysis artifact. Monthly modelling excludes partial 2026.

Targets are never imputed. Missing calendar months remain missing; preprocessing statistics are fitted within each training fold.

2. Analytical workflow

Pinned source→Schema checks→Exact calendar lags→Expanding validation→2025 test→Static website

Lag, rolling, seasonal and airport features use only observations before the target month. Model and document figures are generated from one canonical JSON artifact.

3. Model design

Regression

Ridge, Decision Tree, Random Forest and Histogram Gradient Boosting are compared with previous-month, previous-year seasonal and airport-mean baselines. Candidate settings are selected on expanding 2022–2024 folds; 2025 is evaluated once. Metrics: MAE, RMSE, R², WAPE and median absolute error.

Classification

Logistic Regression, CART and Random Forest predict whether year-on-year growth reaches 10%. Selection uses validation balanced accuracy. Reported test metrics include accuracy, balanced accuracy, precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC, average precision and real confusion counts.

Clustering and PCA

K-Means evaluates k = 2–6. Viable solutions need at least five airports per cluster; among scores within 0.02 of the best silhouette, the smaller k is preferred. PCA visualises the same six scaled features.

4. Interpretation limits

  • Airport passenger movements count arrivals and departures, not unique travellers.
  • Operational-airport counts, traffic-reporting airports and newly constructed airports are different concepts.
  • Low observed traffic does not independently establish infrastructure underutilisation or cause.
  • Missing international movements do not imply that foreign flights are legally forbidden.
  • The one-step forecast assumes actual history becomes available each month; it is not a recursive multi-month forecast.
  • 2020–2021 observations reflect the pandemic shock and may affect temporal validation.
